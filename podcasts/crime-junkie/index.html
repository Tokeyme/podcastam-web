About Crime Junkie

Crime Junkie is a true crime podcast from Audiochuck, hosted by Ashley Flowers with her best friend Brit Prawat. By its own description, the show covers both well-known and underreported cases, including cold cases, missing persons and people in their communities who push for justice. Most episode titles open with a label for the kind of case, such as MURDERED, MISSING, MYSTERIOUS DEATH OF, INFAMOUS or UPDATE.

Recent episodes include a 2009 case in Washington, D.C., in which police have never identified who killed restaurateur Nori Amaya; the 1997 disappearance in Wichita Falls of 20-year-old Keith Mann, whose family is still looking for answers; and the 1983 killing of Toby Tate in San Luis Obispo County, a case two detectives took up again decades later. One story runs across two parts: the 2016 death of Alyssa Romine-Olson in Gilbert, Arizona. An update returns to the Sodder children, five siblings who disappeared after a house fire on Christmas morning in 1945, with new information from a family descendant.

One recent episode, labeled “CJ Spiral,” takes a different form: the hosts react to the documentary “Death of the Pastor's Wife,” and Ashley brings research beyond what the film covers about the case of Mica Miller. Many episode notes list contact details for the agencies handling a case and ask anyone with information to come forward, and the show posts full source lists online. It suits listeners who want cases laid out piece by piece, including ones that have drawn little attention elsewhere.

Questions about Crime Junkie

What is Crime Junkie about?

Crime Junkie retells true crime cases, from well-known ones to cases that have received less coverage. Recent episodes cover murders, disappearances and deaths that families or others have questioned.

Who hosts Crime Junkie?

Ashley Flowers hosts the show with her best friend Brit Prawat. It is presented by Audiochuck Media Company.

What kind of cases does Crime Junkie cover?

Most titles mark the case as a murder, a missing person, a mysterious death, an infamous case or an update. Recent stories include the Sodder children, who disappeared in 1945, and Alyssa Romine-Olson, who died in Gilbert, Arizona, in 2016.
