About The Daily

The Daily is a news podcast from The New York Times. Its hosts are Michael Barbaro, Rachel Abrams and Natalie Kitroeff. Many episodes pair one of the hosts with a Times journalist who has reported on the subject, and the two talk the story through together.

Politics was a steady thread in the run-up to the November 2026 midterm elections. Late October episodes covered investigative reporter Kenneth P. Vogel's account of how people around President Trump turned pardons into a business; a Times investigation into how Alaska's Senate ballot came to list two candidates named Dan Sullivan, and the part Democrats played in it; and Republican candidates who were campaigning as critics of the president. The feed also carries interview episodes, such as a conversation with the writer-director Martin McDonagh about his belief that film can change lives.

Episodes billed as “The Sunday Daily” turn to culture and everyday life. Those from late September through October 2026 looked at Halloween spending on candy, costumes and decorations, Celine Dion's return to performing in Paris, the money pressures pushing restaurant owners to rethink how they operate, and a Times survey of industry insiders that produced a list of the century's top TV series. Other episodes bring in a former Times music critic on how running to music changed the way he listens, and two Times critics on Nathan Fielder's documentary about Elizabeth Holmes. The mix suits listeners who want politics and investigations alongside arts, food and entertainment.
