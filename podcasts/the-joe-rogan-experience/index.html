About The Joe Rogan Experience

The Joe Rogan Experience is the official podcast of comedian Joe Rogan, and Apple lists it under comedy. Episodes are numbered, and titles usually name the guests, though the episodes with the hosts of “Legion of Skanks” and “Mystery Boys” are titled with their podcast's name. The notes introduce each guest and what they are working on, often a new book, a comedy special or a tour.

Comedians appear often in the recent lineup: Jimmy Carr, who has a new book about how jokes work; Colum Tyrrell; Luis J. Gomez, Big Jay Oakerson and Steve Rannazzisi of “Legion of Skanks”; and Kurt Metzger and Duncan Trussell of “Mystery Boys.” Francis Foster and Konstantin Kisin, a comedian and a political commentator who host “TRIGGERnometry,” also sat down for an episode. Beyond comedy, recent guests include actor Michael Douglas and television writer and producer Chuck Lorre, both with new books; journalist Mariana van Zeller, around the premiere of her National Geographic special “Super Scam Me”; ultra-marathon sea swimmer Ross Edgley; author Cody Tucker, whose latest book traces the origins of common words and phrases; and Bill Thompson, a retired chief warrant officer from the U.S. Army who founded a company that makes mapping tools for hunters.

A separately numbered strand, the JRE MMA Show, centers on mixed martial arts. Its 187th edition features Robert Whittaker, a UFC fighter in the light heavyweight division. The show suits listeners who like hearing guests from very different worlds, from stand-up and Hollywood to combat sports and endurance swimming.

Questions about The Joe Rogan Experience

What is The Joe Rogan Experience about?

It is comedian Joe Rogan's official podcast, made up of conversations with a changing cast of guests. Recent episodes feature comedians, an actor, authors, an MMA fighter, a journalist and a retired Army officer.

What kind of guests appear on The Joe Rogan Experience?

Comedians appear often, including Jimmy Carr and the hosts of “Legion of Skanks” and “Mystery Boys.” Other recent guests include actor Michael Douglas, television producer Chuck Lorre, swimmer and coach Ross Edgley and journalist Mariana van Zeller.

What is the JRE MMA Show?

It is a separately numbered set of episodes that centers on mixed martial arts. Episode 187 features Robert Whittaker, who fights at light heavyweight in the UFC.

How often does The Joe Rogan Experience release new episodes?

The public feed of The Joe Rogan Experience lists 15 episodes from the 30 days up to November 1, 2026 — several times a week. The most recent one came out on October 31, 2026.

How long are episodes of The Joe Rogan Experience?

Most recent episodes run about 162 minutes; across the last 30 they ranged from 128 to 187 minutes.
