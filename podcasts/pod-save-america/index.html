About Pod Save America

Pod Save America describes itself as a no-nonsense political conversation. Its hosts, Jon Favreau, Dan Pfeiffer, Jon Lovett and Tommy Vietor, are introduced in the show's description as former Obama aides. The show says it sets out to explain the week's political news, separate what matters from what does not, and suggest ways listeners can help. It comes from Crooked Media.

Many episodes start with two or three of the hosts reacting to the latest news and end with an interview. In the weeks before the November 2026 midterm elections, episodes focused on Senate and governor races, campaign stops, polling and listener questions about the vote. Interview guests have included Democratic candidates such as Chris Pappas, the party's 2026 Senate nominee in New Hampshire, and Keisha Lance Bottoms, its 2026 nominee for governor in Georgia.

The feed also includes conversations with analysts and political figures. CNN's chief data analyst Harry Enten goes through the final polls before Election Day; Amy Walter of the Cook Political Report, where she is publisher and editor-in-chief, assesses the political climate; political consultant David Axelrod discusses the closing arguments Democratic candidates were making; and former Attorney General Eric Holder talks with Alex Wagner about the midterm vote and about redistricting. The show is made for listeners who follow politics closely and want the hosts' take on each week's developments.

Questions about Pod Save America

What is Pod Save America about?

It describes itself as a no-nonsense conversation about politics that breaks down the week's news. In the weeks before the November 2026 midterms, episodes focused on the elections, polling and the campaigns for Senate and governor.

Who hosts Pod Save America?

The show is hosted by Jon Favreau, Dan Pfeiffer, Jon Lovett and Tommy Vietor, whom its description calls former Obama aides. Guests join them regularly.

Who appears as a guest on Pod Save America?

According to the show, politicians, journalists, activists, entertainers and world leaders join regularly. Guests in the weeks before the 2026 midterms included Harry Enten, Amy Walter, David Axelrod, Eric Holder and several Democratic candidates.
