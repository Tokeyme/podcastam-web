Top True Crime Podcasts in the US

True crime podcasts tell the stories of real cases: killings, disappearances, frauds and the investigations and trials that follow. Some come from newsrooms and rely on interviews with detectives, prosecutors, defense lawyers and families. Others follow a single case across an entire season, and still others blend careful research with conversation, humor, local history or tales of the unexplained. Two of the shows below also ask listeners for help: Crime Junkie's notes often direct anyone with information to the agency handling a case, and Bear Brook invites tips by email.

The ten shows below follow Apple's US chart for the True Crime genre as of November 1, 2026, in chart order. Each summary draws only on the show's own description and its episode notes up to that date, which keeps the focus on format, subject matter and the cases covered at the time.

Questions about True Crime podcasts

What is the top true crime podcast in the US?

According to Apple's US chart on November 1, 2026, the top true crime podcast was "Crime Junkie." Ashley Flowers hosts it with her best friend, Brit Prawat, and together they cover well-known and lesser-known cases, including cold cases and missing persons.

Which of these true crime podcasts follow one story across a season?

Bear Brook, Betrayal and Love Trapped are serialized. Bear Brook's third season, "A Private Matter," follows a missing Christmas tree farmer in rural New Hampshire; Betrayal's sixth season, "The Confidante," traces a year of harassment within a circle of friends; and Love Trapped follows Clayton Echard's paternity case through to Laura Owens's sentencing in Arizona.
