Top Society & Culture Podcasts in the US

Society and culture podcasts are about people and the ways they live together. The category is wide: it holds interview shows with public figures, reported series about schools or neighborhoods, explainers on strange corners of history, and personal accounts of family, identity and recovery. Some lean toward ideas and current debates, others toward storytelling, humor or self-reflection, but most share a curiosity about why people do what they do.

The ten shows on this page follow Apple's US chart for Society & Culture podcasts as of November 1, 2026, in the order they appeared there. Each profile below draws on the show's own description and its episode notes up to that date, so it reflects how the producers present their work. The mix runs from personal storytelling and interview shows, among them conversations with people building new technology, to reported series on education and on New York City.

After a first season on why so many kids struggle with reading, the second season moves on to teaching itself, tracing education outsider Zig Engelmann and the method known as Direct Instruction.

Questions about Society & Culture podcasts

What is the top Society & Culture podcast in the US?

According to Apple's US chart on November 1, 2026, the top Society & Culture podcast was The Ezra Klein Show from New York Times Opinion, in which Ezra Klein talks with guests about questions ranging from climate change to the 2026 midterm elections.

Which podcast on this list is about how children learn?

Sold a Story, an investigative series from APM Reports hosted by journalist Emily Hanford. Its first season looked at why so many children struggle to learn to read, and its second season turns to teaching itself, following Zig Engelmann and the method called Direct Instruction.
