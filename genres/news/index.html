Top News Podcasts in the US

News podcasts give the day's events more room than a headline can. Some are brisk briefings that sum up the main stories, some let one reporter walk listeners through a single story from start to finish, and others are talk shows where hosts and guests argue over what the news means. The category spans newsroom productions as well as commentary programs that speak from a stated point of view, so the same week of events can sound very different from one show to the next.

The ten shows on this page follow Apple's US chart for the News genre as of November 1, 2026, in chart order. Each profile is drawn from the show's own description and its episode notes up to that date where those were available, and commentary programs are summarized in the terms they use for themselves.

Top 10 News podcasts

  1. 1

    The Daily

    The New York Times

    This New York Times show names Rachel Abrams, Michael Barbaro and Natalie Kitroeff as its hosts. Episodes often bring in a Times reporter to unpack one story, such as the two Dan Sullivans on Alaska's Senate ballot or the paper's reporting on how the pardon process has been turned into a business. In October 2026, Sunday editions leaned toward culture, from Halloween candy to Celine Dion's return to the stage.

    Typical episode: about 38 min · 10 episodes from the last 30 days in its feed

    Pod Save America

    Tommy Vietor, Jon Lovett, Dan Pfeiffer and Jon Favreau, all former Obama aides, host this political conversation, which says it sorts through the week's news to help listeners see what matters and where they can pitch in. Ahead of the 2026 midterms, the feed featured guests such as CNN data analyst Harry Enten, former Attorney General Eric Holder and Amy Walter of the Cook Political Report.

    Up First from NPR

    NPR

    NPR's morning news show gathers the three main stories of the day, drawing on reporting and analysis from the NPR newsroom. Steve Inskeep, Leila Fadel, A Martinez and Michel Martin are among the hosts it lists, and Ayesha Rascoe leads a longer Sunday edition called "The Sunday Story." Late October 2026 episodes covered midterm campaign spending, Israel's election and a Federal Reserve meeting on interest rates.

    Typical episode: about 13 min · 31 episodes from the last 30 days in its feed

    Follow in the Podcasts AM app: Up First from NPR

    Latest: “Who decides the future of America?” · Nov 1, 2026

    The Megyn Kelly Show

    SiriusXM

    Megyn Kelly leads the main episodes of this SiriusXM show, which promises open and provocative conversations with political, legal and cultural figures and says it has no agenda. Recent guests included Mike Solana, Mark Halperin and Michael Knowles, while AM Update editions run through the morning's headlines. The feed also carries segments from MK True Crime shows such as "Positively Legal," each with its own hosts.

    Typical episode: about 103 min · 47 episodes from the last 30 days in its feed

Questions about News podcasts

What is the top news podcast in the US?

"The Daily" from The New York Times was in first place among news podcasts, according to Apple's US chart on November 1, 2026. The show often brings in a Times reporter to explain a single story.

Which news podcasts on this list come from NPR?

Two of them. "Up First from NPR" gathers the day's main stories with reporting and analysis from the NPR newsroom, and "NPR News Now" offers a brief roundup of the latest headlines that its feed says is updated every hour.
