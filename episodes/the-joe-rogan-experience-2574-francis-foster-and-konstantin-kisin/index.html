What this episode is about

Episode #2574 of The Joe Rogan Experience brings in two guests who run a podcast of their own. Francis Foster, a comedian, and Konstantin Kisin, a political commentator, host "TRIGGERnometry" together, and here they appear as guests instead of hosts, in an episode that runs about two hours and 44 minutes.

Both also have work of their own. Foster hosts a second show, "The Francis Foster Show," and wrote "(Un)educated," a book about his life as a teacher; its subtitle warns readers away from the profession. Kisin wrote "An Immigrant's Love Letter to the West." Apart from links to the guests' YouTube channels and websites, the episode notes stop at these introductions and do not list the subjects of the conversation.
