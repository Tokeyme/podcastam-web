What this episode is about

"Justice for Faith" comes from 20/20, a true crime podcast from ABC News, and it follows a case that began when a college student was murdered in her apartment off campus. According to the episode notes, the work on the case stretched over a decade. Those working it faced a pool of thousands of people who could have been responsible, and they collected hundreds of DNA samples as they tried to narrow it down.

The notes describe that work as painstaking and say it finally led to a verdict, without saying what the verdict was. Beyond that, they keep to the broad outline of the case: the size of the suspect pool, the number of DNA samples and the years the work took.

In this episode

  • The murder of a college student in her off-campus apartment
  • An investigation facing thousands of potential suspects
  • Hundreds of DNA samples gathered along the way
  • Over a decade of careful work before a verdict

About the show

The 20/20 podcast from ABC News describes itself as a home for true crime mysteries, exclusive interviews with people in the news, investigative reports and detailed coverage of major stories. Alongside its main episodes, the feed carries an After Show, episodes from a True Crime Vault and a spotlight on true crime originals.
