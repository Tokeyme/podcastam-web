What this episode is about

Ahead of Election Day, co-host Tommy Vietor sits down with Harry Enten, CNN's chief data analyst, for a final look at the numbers, in an episode that runs just over an hour. According to the episode notes, the two weigh what they see as a rising chance of a lopsided Democratic result in the House, take up how far the polls can be trusted and lay out what to keep an eye on as results arrive on Tuesday night.

They also turn to the Senate contests the notes call nail-biting and to states that have, in the show's telling, surprisingly come into play. Then Enten talks about what it's like being, in the episode's own phrase, "Trump's favorite pollster."

In this episode

  • Guest Harry Enten, CNN's chief data analyst
  • The House picture and a possible lopsided Democratic result
  • How much trust to place in the polls
  • Close Senate races and states the show calls surprising
  • What to watch as results come in Tuesday night
  • Enten on being "Trump's favorite pollster"

About the show

Pod Save America describes itself as a frank conversation about politics. Its four hosts, Tommy Vietor, Jon Favreau, Dan Pfeiffer and Jon Lovett, all previously worked as aides to Obama, and the show says it aims to unpack each week's news and help listeners sort out what is important and how to get involved. The show says guests regularly include journalists, activists, politicians, entertainers and world leaders.
