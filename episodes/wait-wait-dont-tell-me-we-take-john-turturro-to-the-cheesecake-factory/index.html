What this episode is about

Actor John Turturro is the featured guest on this edition of NPR's weekly news quiz, and the episode notes single out one moment in particular: his impression of Christopher Walken. The title, "We take John Turturro to the Cheesecake Factory," plays on a subject that also comes up when the panel gets talking.

The panel on this episode is Faith Salie, Hari Kondabolu and Paula Poundstone. According to the notes, their conversation covers the Cheesecake Factory along with learning to love a man made out of wicker, a teaser the description leaves unexplained. As the show describes itself, it offers a chance to laugh and test what you know with comedians and a celebrity guest, all built around the week's news.
