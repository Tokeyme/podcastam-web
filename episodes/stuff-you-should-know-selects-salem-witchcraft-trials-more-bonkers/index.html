What this episode is about

This "Selects" release brings back an earlier Stuff You Should Know episode, which the notes call a classic, about the Salem witchcraft trials. The notes open with a problem of hindsight: some three centuries later, it is tempting to settle on sweeping explanations for why the people of Salem Village persecuted their neighbors and sent some of them to their deaths.

The notes suggest the episode tries to avoid that kind of shortcut. While the trials have turned into a staple of American history, the notes say the reasons Salem lost its mind are still not understood. The hosts set out to sort what they think is known about the events from what is not, and the title promises a story stranger than most people expect.

In this episode

  • Why Salem villagers persecuted and executed their neighbors
  • The risk of oversimplifying events from three centuries away
  • A story the title calls "More Bonkers Than You Know"
  • What is thought to be known, and what remains unclear
